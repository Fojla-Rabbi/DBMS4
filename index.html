ABOUT SHURANGAN

Rooted in culture,
built for learning.

Shurangan is a cultural academy designed around the rich artistic heritage of Bangladesh. Students learn through regular classes, practice, assessment, performances and workshops.

Our curriculum includes Rabindra Sangeet, Nazrul Geeti, Bangladeshi folk music, classical and folk dance, recitation, theatre and visual arts.
