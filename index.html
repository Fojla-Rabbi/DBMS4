ABOUT SHURANGAN

Heritage in practice,
creativity in motion.

Shurangan is a modern cultural academy built around the artistic heritage of Bangladesh. We combine structured training, regular assessment, performances and a strong teacher–student community.

Students can explore Rabindra Sangeet, Nazrul Geeti, Baul and folk music, Bengali dance traditions, recitation, theatre and visual arts in a disciplined learning environment.
